## Igor Poltavtsev

Senior iOS engineer, 13 years in native iOS. Warsaw, Poland. Open to long-term contract work.

For seven years I was an iOS engineer on [Unfold](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/unfold-reels-story-maker/id1247275033), Squarespace's storytelling app: 60M+ downloads, 4.8 stars from more than 158,000 App Store reviews. I joined in 2018 as the third iOS engineer, when it was still a startup, and stayed after the acquisition while the team grew to around twenty.

My part of it: the story editor's move from OpenGL to Metal, monetization (StoreKit, RevenueCat, Superwall, price experiments country by country), A/B experiments and remote config, Core Data carried through more than ten schema versions with a migration each, the notification service extension behind rich push, and keeping the app under Apple's 200 MB cellular limit.

Alongside that I was sole developer of [FlexyOrder](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/flexyorder/id1553871441), the staff ordering app of a Danish hospitality platform, from 2017 to 2026, and of the [Smarty Ears](https://apps.apple.com/us/developer/smarty-ears/id348779167) catalogue of 20+ speech therapy apps for iPad.

**Stack:** Swift, Objective-C, SwiftUI, UIKit, Combine, Metal, AVFoundation, Core Data, StoreKit, RevenueCat, Superwall, Firebase, fastlane, GitHub Actions, XCTest. TypeScript and Node.js when a product needs a backend.

Most of my work lives in private client repositories and in the App Store rather than here.
